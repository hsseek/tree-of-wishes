Keyboard shortcuts

On a computer, press the ? key anytime to open the shortcut guide. The available keys:

?Show this help
jOlder wish
kYounger wish
rOpen a random wish
/Focus search
Ctrl+EnterPlace a wish (in the Make a Wish form)
EscClose

On a phone, use the arrow buttons at the top of an open wish to move between wishes.
